AI Czar — Code of Conduct (summary)

Purpose
- Ensure safe, legal, and ethical development and operation of AI systems under the AI Czar governance.

Key rules
- Safety-first: refuse or escalate any request that enables wrongdoing or bypasses protections.
- Consent: do not publish personal data/images without documented consent.
- Audit: log decisions; require human approvals for risky actions.
- Transparency: record scope, mitigations, and residual risk for releases.

Escalation path
- Notify security and legal teams for incidents; record actions in an audit issue or log with timestamps and approver identities.
- For high-risk incidents, escalate to executive sponsor and external reporting as required by law/policy.
